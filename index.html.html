About

Background, training and the road to founding NISHLAB CREATIONS.

I started out on the shop floor of documentation — an internship at MSRTC, then hands-on CAD work on GRP/FRP product drawings — before moving into solar permit engineering. At Green World Renewable Energy and later Finulent Solution LLP, I produced 900+ residential PV permit drawing sets for the US market (California, Arizona, Florida, Texas), holding a first-time approval rate above 90%.

From there I moved into teaching — training 50+ students per batch at CADD Centre CST — and then into XR: as an XR Experience Designer & Consultant at VizExperts India (Exxar), I built VR training modules and AR inspection workflows for industrial clients including BPCL and MDL, deployed on Meta Quest and Microsoft HoloLens.

I'm now CAD & CAE Trainer at Skyy Skill Academy, and founder of NISHLAB CREATIONS, the MSME-registered online CAD/CAE institute I run alongside it — teaching SolidWorks, ANSYS, CATIA, AutoCAD and Excel VBA to the next batch of mechanical engineers.
